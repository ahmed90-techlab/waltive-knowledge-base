Quality differences & common issues

The source material identifies five recurrent checks: delamination, hot-weather warping, batch-to-batch colour mismatch, edge brittleness and poor scratch/wear resistance.

Control before shipment

  • Approve a retained production sample with full finish and profile details.
  • Set measurable acceptance criteria for dimensions, colour, surface and packaging.
  • Inspect production and pre-shipment lots against the agreed sample.
