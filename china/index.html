Three major production hubs

Shandong

Linyi is a core hub with broad-volume manufacturing and strong price competitiveness.

Foshan

Guangdong production is closely associated with decorative development and finish variety.

Zhejiang

Jinhua, Haining and Jiaxing form an important cluster for engineered panel capability.
