Competitor objection handling

Price objections

Reconfirm that construction, finish, inspection standard, packaging and commercial terms are comparable; then explain the value difference with evidence.

Reliability objections

Offer proof: approved samples, production plan, inspection checkpoints and realistic delivery milestones.

Customization objections

Clarify the buyer’s requirement, MOQ and change-control process before committing to a finish or profile.
